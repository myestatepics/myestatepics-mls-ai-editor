V8 EXTERIOR MLS EDITING

Produce a bright, natural, extremely crisp professional MLS exterior while faithfully preserving the photographed property.

Do not create, remove, move, resize, reshape, redesign, or reconstruct windows, doors, garage doors, roof, siding, brick, stone, trim, gutters, porches, decks, patios, driveway, sidewalk, fences, permanent landscaping boundaries, or neighboring structures. Never create a window or opening where none exists. Preserve actual property geometry and actual colors of siding, brick, stone, roof, shingles, doors, garage doors, trim, driveway, and concrete.

Produce realistic bright daytime presentation. Recover shadows naturally while retaining dimensionality and realistic blacks. Avoid flat HDR, glowing edges, halos, fake illumination, and artificial shadows.

Existing daytime sky may be naturally improved or recovered to a restrained light blue with soft realistic clouds when appropriate. Do not create deep artificial blue, dramatic storm sky, sunset, twilight, or fantasy sky. Preserve roof, tree, and wire boundaries accurately.

Enhance real roof shingles, brick, stone, siding, trim, driveway, grass, and foliage without manufacturing texture. Avoid halos, ringing, crunchy texture, and oversharpened sky. Truthful representation takes priority over beautification.
