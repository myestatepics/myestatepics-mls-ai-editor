MYESTATEPICS MLS INTERIOR — PRODUCTION BASE PROMPT v1.6

PHOTO CORRECTION ONLY

The original photograph is the absolute source of truth. Create a professional
MLS interior enhancement that looks like a carefully edited Lightroom image.

Do not redesign, restage, add, remove, replace, move, or reshape anything.
Do not alter architecture, furniture, fixtures, room geometry, window geometry,
reflections, or exterior structures.

PRIORITY ORDER

1. Preserve exact material identity and room geometry.
2. Establish strictly neutral white balance with no global cast.
3. Produce naturally bright MLS exposure while retaining depth.
4. Recover crisp, realistic exterior detail through windows.
5. Make genuine visible open sky soft natural light blue.

MATERIAL COLOR LOCK

Preserve the photographed hue and relative color of walls, ceilings, trim,
cabinets, countertops, flooring, tile, stone, wood, carpet, rugs, furniture,
fabric, and fixtures.

Beige remains beige. Greige remains greige. Cream remains cream. Warm wood
remains warm wood. Dark cabinets remain their photographed color. Do not
whiten, gray, cool, warm, enrich, or desaturate materials.

STRICTLY NEUTRAL WHITE BALANCE

Neutral walls, ceilings, trim, white cabinetry, and white countertops must
render neutral—not yellow, orange, green, magenta, blue, cyan, or cold gray.

Remove any unwanted global color cast, but do not replace one cast with
another. Preserve genuinely warm bulbs and warm materials locally without
allowing that warmth to spread across the whole room.

EXPOSURE AND DEPTH

Brighten only as much as needed for a professional MLS result. Use balanced
exposure, shadow recovery, and midtone recovery. Preserve black depth and
architectural dimensionality. Avoid glowing walls, clipped ceilings, gray
shadows, flat HDR tonality, haze, or synthetic rendering.

WINDOW DETAIL

Recover real exterior detail present in the source: brick, siding, roofs,
window frames, trees, grass, landscaping, and neighboring structures.

Exterior detail should be crisp, clear, and naturally contrasted—not soft,
foggy, washed out, or covered by cyan haze. Preserve clean edges without
halos or artificial oversharpening.

LIGHT-BLUE SKY THROUGH WINDOWS

Where genuine open sky is visible through a window, that open-sky area must
appear as a subtle, natural, light daytime blue.

Apply blue only to genuine open-sky pixels. Never tint window glass as a whole,
reflections, buildings, roofs, siding, brick, trees, grass, landscaping,
window frames, curtains, walls, ceilings, cabinetry, countertops, or furniture.

If uncertain whether an area is sky or structure, preserve the photographed
color and do not apply blue. Do not invent buildings, trees, landscaping,
clouds, or other exterior scenery.

OUTPUT QUALITY

Retain natural texture, fine detail, and photographic realism. Avoid painterly,
rendered, plastic, AI-generated, HDR-heavy, hazy, or overprocessed appearance.
Do not add text, logos, watermarks, borders, or artificial framing.
